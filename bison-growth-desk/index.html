Proposed starting fee$850Negotiable scope & fee · 21-day Bucknell pilot

The growth problem is not awareness alone. A sports marketplace becomes useful only when a student can open the app and find the right game, at the right time, with enough players. The Campus Launch Lab is designed to create and measure that activity.

Days 1–4

Find the friction

Review the product and current acquisition data, interview initial users, and choose priority sports and campus channels.

Days 5–10

Build the supply

Recruit 10–15 student hosts, prepare launch assets, and schedule the first wave of pickup sessions.

Days 11–17

Run the experiment

Promote sessions, track attendance, test referral messages, and collect structured participant feedback.

Days 18–21

Package the system

Evaluate the funnel and unit economics, then hand over the dashboard, host playbook and next-campus recommendations.
