A few useful answers

Before we get started.

Do you guarantee recovered revenue?

No. The sprint creates a tested process for managing opportunities. Results depend on lead quality, timing, your offer, and how the system is used.

Is this dashboard a real CRM?

The dashboard above is an interactive demonstration with fictional leads. A client workflow is scoped and built separately, with approved tools and access.

Will you automatically message my customers?

Only within an agreed client implementation, using approved templates, appropriate consent, and connected tools. This website's demo never sends messages.

What happens after I submit?

Your inquiry is submitted through Formspree to our shared studio inbox, with a Revenue Recovery Studio label. To schedule a consultation, use the calendar link and complete Cal.com’s booking confirmation.

Privacy

Your inquiry, handled with care.

We collect the name, work email, business, selected service, and message you submit to respond to your request. Formspree processes and stores submissions in the studio’s shared account. Cal.com handles booking details under its own privacy policy. Please do not submit sensitive customer information. To request access or deletion, reply to the studio’s response.
